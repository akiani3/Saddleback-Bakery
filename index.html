About Our Bakery

At Saddleback Bakery, we believe that great baking requires patience, passion, and uncompromising quality. Established in 2018, our hearth ovens deliver traditional sourdough breads and exquisite artisan treats to our local community.


Every ingredient is selected with care—from organic heritage wheat to real citrus zest and sweet butter. Step inside to taste the warmth of time-honored baking traditions!
