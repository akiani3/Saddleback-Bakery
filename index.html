Case Study #SB-2026

Balancing Artisanal Fermentation Standards with Wholesale Expansion

Section I: Introduction & Industry Context
The contemporary artisanal baking sector operates at a dynamic intersection of traditional craftsmanship, shifting consumer preferences, and rigorous operational demands. As specialty bakeries like Saddleback Bakery navigate competitive market environments, maintaining uncompromised product quality while pursuing enterprise sustainability represents a central strategic challenge.

Section II: Research Methodology & Findings
Master Baker Elena notes that their 72-hour sourdough fermentation process creates incredible crust and flavor, driving high local demand. However, three local gourmet grocers want 300 loaves daily. Current deck oven capacity is capped at 120 loaves per day.

Section III: Strategic Recommendations

  • Phased Production Standardization (Modular SOPs)
  • Customer Journey & Order Staging Optimization
  • Value-Added Menu Engineering & Margin Management
  • Cross-Training Programs Between Baking and Service Teams
